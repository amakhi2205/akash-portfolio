Featured Projects

Dynamic Load Modeling

Measurement-Based Modeling of AI Data-Center GPU Power Fluctuations

Analyzed MIT Supercloud GPU power traces from BERT-based AI workload jobs with Python, pandas, NumPy, matplotlib, and SciPy FFT. The work identified burst-like GPU power behavior and dominant frequency components near 1.44-1.45 Hz and 0.87 Hz.

Open Report

Solar IBR Dynamics

Coordinated Generator and Aggregated Solar IBR Behavior

Studied grid-following solar IBR behavior in PSSE using a 3-bus system and modified IEEE 9-bus test system. The study evaluated WECC REGCA, REECA, and REPCA models, LVRT, PLL response, and voltage support during faults.

Open Report

Transmission Planning

Least-Cost Transmission Design for KWW Wind Farm Interconnection

Evaluated transmission upgrade cases for integrating a 200 MW wind farm into the MLP system. The selected Case 4 design used three 69 kV Condor lines, satisfied N-1 reliability requirements, and produced an estimated net economic impact of $7.36M.

Open Report

Energy Markets

Solar IBR Impacts on Day-Ahead Electricity Markets

Analyzed CAISO solar generation, load, net-load, and LMP data to evaluate midday price suppression, duck-curve behavior, evening ramp conditions, net-load variability, and grid balancing needs.
